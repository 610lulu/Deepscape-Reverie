## About the Project

Deepscape Reverie centers on the individual memories of Shenjing Village, integrating residents' oral histories and spatial memories through digital technology. By mapping the relationships between space, events, and emotions through a knowledge graph, the project translates them into spatial prototypes shaped by light and shadow.

Combining dreamcore-inspired imagery with chapter-based interactive storytelling, the online exhibition invites visitors to explore and participate in reconstructed memories. The project transforms individual recollections into a shared digital memory and investigates new approaches to presenting cultural heritage online.
